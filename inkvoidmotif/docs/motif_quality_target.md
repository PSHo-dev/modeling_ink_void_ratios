# Motif Quality Target

This pipeline uses the collaborator motif-bank image as a quality exemplar, not as source content.

The target behavior is:

- Segment whole reusable motifs, not rectangular crop fragments.
- Preserve the motif's internal brushwork, line density, dry-brush texture, wash gradients, roof geometry, tree needles, rock texture, water bands, and mist edges.
- Keep enough local context for the motif to remain coherent: bridges need adjacent shrubs and masonry, villages need roofs plus trees/walls, mountains need their wash edge and ridge texture.
- Remove unrelated paper/silk ground, labels, seals, borders, and neighboring objects.
- Keep natural hand-painted contours. Avoid hard sticker silhouettes, white halos, square paper patches, and generic repainting.
- Never put text into motif assets. Labels are only for review contact sheets.

For final recomposition:

- Use the motif bank as visual identity, not as vague inspiration.
- Integrate motifs into one continuous Chinese painting on silk or paper.
- Blend transparent cutout edges into mist, water, land, and paper tone.
- Do not create a collage, contact sheet, UI grid, or labeled diagram.
